About Squad CID Foundation

A Movement for Justice & Service to the Nation

The Crime Branch & Investigation Commission (Squad CID Foundation) is a non-profit organisation committed to building safer communities, protecting human rights and supporting people in need.

Through awareness programmes, community outreach, support for vulnerable people and responsible citizen engagement, we work towards a society where every voice matters.

  • Awareness, prevention and public education
  • Human rights, dignity and community welfare
  • Volunteer-led service and local partnerships

National President & National Chairman

G. Sunil Gulecha Jain, BBA

G. Sunil Gulecha Jain leads the foundation as its National President and National Chairman. Under his leadership the foundation works under the motto printed on its badge: Truth, Integrity, Justice.

The foundation's stated focus is crime-intelligence awareness, public safety education and helping citizens bring their concerns to the right authorities. To reach the chairman's office, call 91235 95901 or write to SQUAD.CID007@GMAIL.COM.
